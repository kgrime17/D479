Hiking


Taniti has many hiking trails leading to popular vista points with hikes ranging from an 11-mile trail to the top of our volcano, to a 4-mile trail looping around near town with several wildflower fields brightening the trail.
Or simply enjoy a walk along our beaches and enjoy the clear ocean view!

Our Most Popular Hiking Trails

#1 King's Road Trail | 4.9 Miles | Moderate Difficulty
Make your way to the base of our volcano, Mount Crimson, featuring beautiful views within the tropical rainforest and ending on a short plateau overlooking the entire North side of the island, with the volcano silhouetting your back!

#2 Hidden Cascades Trail | 8.2 Miles | Hard Difficulty
Travel along the coastline, witnessing the waves crash along a smooth shore before turning into the rainforest and climbing to a secluded waterfall deep within the island. A clearing further along makes this a popular destination for a picnic break at the end of the trail!

#3 Rocky Road | 8.9 Miles | Moderate Difficulty
This hiking trail takes you along the Eastern edge of the island, letting you witness the beautiful variety of our coastline while walking along the rocky edges.

#4 Mount Crimson Climb | 11.8 Miles | Hard Difficulty
Climb up the infamous volcano to witness its activity firsthand! The trail climbs to the top of Mount Crimson, providing a clear and safe view into the caldera. Guided hikes available!

#5 Faerie's Loop | 4.8 Miles | Easy Difficulty
This sweet little trail loops through the rainforests near the main resort. Enjoy a stunning view of the natural landscape, including several picturesque flower fields along the trail.

Restaurants


There are currently 10 restaurants for tourists across the island. A majority of them serve local fish and rice, fresh from that day's catches. Though some restaurants also serve American-style or Pan-Asain meals to give folks a taste of home.
