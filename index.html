Welcome!

This is the official Taniti Tourism website. We welcome guests of all ages, backgrounds, and interests. Use this website as your guide to explore what Tahiti has to offer and how you can book your next trip!
Taniti is a small, tropical island located in the Pacific. Home to varied terrain, which includes both sandy and rocky beaches, a small but safe harbor, lush tropical rainforests, and a mountainous interior that even includes a small, active volcano.
Taniti also hosts a local history museum, fishing tours, snorkeling, and more to excite every island visitor!
