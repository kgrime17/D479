Transport to Taniti

Taniti is home to both a small airport for air travel and commercial docks for tourists traveling by cruise!
Visitors can book a plane ticket to travel by small jets or propeller planes and land within the city.
Alternatively, visitors taking a cruise across the Pacific may dock within the Yellow Leaf Bay every Friday.
